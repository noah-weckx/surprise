# Summer Horticulture Opportunities in Flanders (Geel / Balen / Kempen Region)

## 1. Top Regional Internship Opportunities

- **Thomas More (Campus Geel)**
  - *RADIUS Expertise Center*: Sustainable biomass, microalgae cultivation, bio-based fertilizers, and circular nutrient recycling.
  - *Experimental Greenhouses & Climate Chambers*: Controlled-environment agriculture (CEA), LED light spectrum optimization, automated irrigation, and hydroponics or aeroponics.
- **KU Leuven (Campus Geel)**
  - *SusCroPP (Sustainable Crop Production and Protection)*: Biological pest management, plant-pathogen interactions, bio-stimulants, and soil durability.
  - *IP&P (Insect Production and Processing)*: Insect farming for bioconversion, secondary nutrient recovery, and biological control agents.
- **VITO (Vlaamse Instelling voor Technologisch Onderzoek) – Mol**
  - *Remote Sensing & Phenotyping*: Drone and satellite hyperspectral imaging for crop health, canopy stress, nitrogen status, and spatial yield modeling.
  - *Agro-Data Science*: Spatial agri-data platforms and environmental monitoring networks, including EMPHASIS-Belgium.
- **Biobest Belgium – Westerlo**
  - *R&D & Entomological Testing*: Beneficial insects, predatory mites, biopesticides, and bumblebee pollination systems.
  - The primary R&D environment is English-speaking.
- **Hooibeekhoeve – Geel**
  - *Field Crop & Soil Dynamics*: Soil compaction, precision organic fertilization, and cover cropping.
  - *Water & Resource Efficiency*: Rainwater capture, slurry processing, and closed-loop farm water filtration.
- **Harvestis / Proefcentrum Hoogstraten (PCH)**
  - *Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA)*: Greenhouse strawberries, hydroponic leafy greens, tomatoes, and alternative substrate testing.
  - PCH is a lead partner in EU research consortia and offers English placements.

## 2. Application Timelines & Action Steps

Planning ahead can make a real difference, especially when research teams are arranging summer projects and paperwork. These recommended outreach windows are a helpful starting point:

| Institution | Primary Outreach Target | Recommended Contact Window | Key Administrative Step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thomas More (Campus Geel)** | RADIUS Lab Lead / International Office | Dec–Feb | Thomas More Voluntary Internship Contract |
| **KU Leuven (Campus Geel)** | Bioscience PIs (SusCroPP / IP&P) | Nov–Jan (5 mo. lead time) | KU Leuven Research Internship Portal submission |
| **VITO (Mol)** | Remote Sensing Unit / HR Portal | Nov–Feb | Spontaneous Internship Application via portal |

Wishing you the best as you explore these opportunities. A thoughtful early message about your interests, experience, and summer availability is a great way to begin.
